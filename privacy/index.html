Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

7Media Venture ("we", "us") provides video and photography production services for local businesses. This page explains how we handle information in connection with our internal tools and this website.

Information we collect

We collect basic business contact information (business name, website, and a publicly listed contact email) for companies we may reach out to about our services. We do not collect this information from visitors to this website beyond standard, non-identifying web server logs.

How we use it

Contact information is used solely to send outreach about our video/photography services and to manage our own internal records (spreadsheets, scheduling tools). We do not sell or share this information with third parties.

Internal tools

We use Google Workspace tools (such as Google Sheets) internally to organize our outreach. Access to these tools is restricted to 7Media Venture staff and is authorized via Google's OAuth sign-in, which only grants access to data we already own.

Opt-out

Anyone can ask to be removed from our contact list at any time by replying "stop" to any email from us, or by contacting us using the details below.
